Privacy Policy

Locate+ · Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. About this policy

Locate+ (the “App”) is an iOS app for manually sharing one location at a time with the people you allow. This policy explains what the App processes, where it is stored and the choices you have. The App is provided by the Locate+ developer (“we”). Contact: https://github.com/xxlcmb9/doc/issues.

2. Information we process

  • Share number — a random 9-digit number created on your device. No email, phone number, password or social account is required, and we do not ask for your name, contacts or photos.
  • Location you confirm — latitude, longitude, altitude and timestamps, sent only when you tap share and accept the confirmation.
  • Allow list and block list — the share numbers you allow or block, stored in your own profile.
  • Messages you send us — if you send us a message through the support page.

3. How sharing works

  • Nothing is published automatically, in the background or on a timer. Every check-in needs your explicit confirmation and can be cancelled.
  • Each check-in sends one record that replaces your previous record. The App keeps no location history.
  • The record expires in the App one hour after publication, and other users then no longer see it. Expiry is a display rule only: it does not delete the file from cloud storage. The file is deleted when you withdraw the share or delete your identity.
  • Showing your own position on the local map is a separate optional setting and shares nothing.

4. Where data is stored

Shared records, allow/block lists and the number index are stored in Tencent Cloud Object Storage (COS), ap-nanjing region, mainland China, using temporary credentials issued by the App's authentication endpoint. Your number and lists are also stored on your device. We do not run a separate analytics database for your locations. After deletion, provider backups may retain copies for a limited period.

5. Third-party services

  • Tencent Cloud COS — storage of shared records and lists.
  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform — ads and consent dialogs; Google may receive device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions.
  • Apple — App Store, in-app purchase and payments. We never receive your card details.
  • open-elevation.com — only the coordinate you pick on the map is sent when you ask for its altitude, and you can cancel first.

We do not sell personal data.

6. Ads and tracking choices

Ads support the free version. On iOS 14.5 and later the App asks for App Tracking Transparency permission where required; if you decline you still see ads, but not personalized ones. You can change your choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and in the in-app consent options.

7. Security and its limits

Traffic uses HTTPS/TLS and expiring credentials. Before any lookup, the App checks the block list and allow list on both sides; blocking wins. This control is enforced by the App and is not isolated on the server, so it cannot stop someone who obtains valid credentials from reading objects in the bucket, and it cannot prevent a recipient from taking screenshots or copies. Please share only what you can afford to disclose.

8. Your choices

  • Withdraw a share at any time — this deletes the shared record.
  • Block or unblock people; unblocking does not restore an allow entry.
  • Hide your own position on the local map.
  • Delete your identity in the App: your location, profile and index entry are removed, then local data. A lifetime purchase is not affected.
  • Revoke location access in iOS Settings; the map, compass and altitude tools then stop working, but the rest of the App remains usable.
  • Contact us through the support link to request access, correction or deletion of data linked to your share number.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has shared data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above will change and the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open a support request at https://github.com/xxlcmb9/doc/issues.
